Last week, first-year students from the course Física: a Terra e o Universo at Técnico Lisboa visited the VOXEL lab as part of their introduction to research in physics.

The visit was led by Gareth Williams, who gave the students a tour of the lab and a glimpse into our work on laser-plasma interactions, X-ray imaging, and extreme states of matter.

Great questions, we hope to see some of them back in the lab in the future!
