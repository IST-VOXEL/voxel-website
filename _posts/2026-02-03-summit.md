VOXEL Laboratory took part in the Técnico Innovation Summit (TIS 2026), showcasing its research and technological capabilities.

The VOXEL stand highlighted the NanoXCAN project, the recent renovation and improvements of the laboratory, and the broader scope of VOXEL’s research in advanced X-ray imaging and instrumentation. The event allowed VOXEL to connect with industry, academia, and the public, sharing knowledge about ongoing projects and experimental approaches.

Furthermore, Marta Fajardo, VOXEL’s PI, participated in a roundtable on Health and Biotechnology, discussing the role of advanced imaging technologies in life sciences and innovation.
