VOXEL Laboratory was visited by a delegation of the Portuguese Air Force, led by the Chief of Staff, General João Cartaxo Alves, as part of an institutional visit to IPFN.

During the visit, VOXEL researchers presented ongoing research activities and technological capabilities developed at the laboratory, highlighting current projects and experimental approaches in advanced X-ray imaging and instrumentation. The discussion focused on scientific results and technological developments with potential relevance to defence-related applications.

The visit provided an opportunity to exchange perspectives on research, innovation, and the role of advanced imaging technologies in addressing challenges in the defence domain.
